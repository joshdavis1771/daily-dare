# Daily Dare rules — draft for agreement

Proposed 2026-09-30. These are recommended product decisions, not assertions about current behavior. Owner: Product lead (provisional). No rules have been approved in this document. The [delivery plan](DELIVERY_PLAN.md) uses these examples as future acceptance cases.

| Rule | Proposed behavior | Example and expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| R1. Day and deadline | Use an account's confirmed IANA home timezone and server time. A dare opens at its persisted random time from 09:00 inclusive to 20:00 exclusive. Deadline is the next local midnight, exclusive. Never calculate a day by adding 24 hours. | Auckland's 23-hour spring-forward day and 25-hour fall-back day both end at the next local midnight. Client clock changes do not alter the deadline. |
| R2. Enrollment and travel | Show and confirm home timezone at enrollment; it stays fixed for beta. Travel/device timezone changes do not change assignments or deadlines. Offer no self-service timezone changes until a migration rule is agreed. First eligible streak day is the next home-calendar date after enrollment, giving a full scheduled opportunity. | Join at 23:55: no five-minute deadline or missed day; first counted dare is tomorrow. Travel from Auckland to London: display the home-timezone deadline, optionally its local equivalent, without extending it. |
| R3. Missed days and streaks | Count consecutive completed eligible dates. Before today's deadline, preserve yesterday's streak. Missing an eligible day breaks the chain at midnight; reopening the app does not erase the miss. Deleted proof does not fabricate a miss: retain a minimal completion ledger until account deletion. No paid skips/restores in beta. | Complete Monday and Tuesday: streak 2 on Wednesday until deadline. Miss Wednesday: streak 0 Thursday; completing Thursday starts at 1. Dates before enrollment never count as misses. |
| R4. Completion and retries | One completion per user/day, transactionally recorded. Server must accept completion while the window is open and only after validated, processed photo proof is ready. Upload start time is insufficient. An idempotent retry returns the original result even after midnight; rejected/unaccepted work cannot be backdated by the client. | Completion accepted at 23:59:59 but response lost: retry at 00:01 returns the same success and streak. Upload starts at 23:59 but processing finishes at 00:01: no completion, unless an audited service-outage exemption applies. |
| R5. Outages and withdrawn dares | An operator may mark an affected user/day exempt after a confirmed service incident or unsafe-dare withdrawal. An exempt date neither increments nor breaks a streak. Preserve recorded completions; keep an immutable reason/actor/incident record. No client-selected forgiveness. Notify affected users; never falsely label exemption as completed. | Streak 3 before an exempt date remains 3; next completed eligible date makes 4. Personal loss of connectivity is not automatically a service incident. A withdrawn dare cannot accept new proof. |
| R6. Friendship and removal | Sharing requires mutual accepted friendship; a one-way follow is insufficient. Removal revokes both parties' friendship-based access to past and future proof immediately. Block also removes friendship and disallows new contact/interactions until unblocked; unblocking does not restore friendship. | A and B are friends and B can view A's shared photo. A removes B: B's next feed/media request is denied, including a previously opened URL. Unrelated C is always denied. |
| R7. Privacy and revocation | Default proof to owner-only, with explicit friends sharing. No public proof in photo beta. Check current authorization for originals, thumbnails and derivatives on every request; avoid bearer URLs that remain usable after revocation. Revocation prevents future server retrieval, not screenshots or copies already obtained. | A changes friends-shared photo to owner-only: B loses access immediately. A stale feed card cannot bypass media authorization. Adding a friend does not automatically expose older owner-only photos. |
| R8. Photos and failed uploads | Decode and verify supported images server-side; enforce 20 MB input limit, correct orientation, resize to a maximum 2048-pixel long edge without upscaling, and re-encode without metadata. Only processed derivatives become viewable. Pending uploads are private and cannot count. Recovery uses a stable upload/submission identity; clean abandoned media after a defined retention period. | JPEG with GPS EXIF yields an oriented image with no GPS/EXIF metadata. A spoofed MIME type is rejected. Interrupted upload can retry without multiple posts; failed processing shows a recoverable error. |
| R9. Notifications and premium | Notifications are optional reminders, independent of assignment/completion. Suppress completed, exempt and expired reminders; retry transient failures within the deadline. Premium must not weaken privacy or make the free completion loop depend on payment. | Denying notifications still allows home-screen discovery and completion. Refunding a future archive subscription removes the paid benefit but leaves ordinary account data and free streak behavior intact. |

## Decisions to confirm

Agree or amend R1–R9 before treating them as implementation requirements. Particularly consequential choices are the fixed home timezone, next-day enrollment, operator exemptions, mutual friendship, owner-only default, immediate revocation, and photo processing before the deadline. These are recommendations rather than inferred consent.

For beta operations, also set abandoned-upload retention, deleted-account backup retention, incident response ownership, and backup recovery objectives before Stage 6 closes. These values are intentionally unresolved rather than silently invented.

## Differences from the current implementation

Current daily SQL fixes timezone on first status call and plans that same date; it has no enrollment confirmation, exemption ledger or withdrawal state. Its streak loop counts only completed days. Completion validates Storage metadata but does not prove image decoding, resizing or metadata removal. Public post/profile reads, one-way follows and owner-only Storage do not implement the proposed friendship-sharing contract. Existing demo restoration and Plus token behavior do not define the production rules.

Implement changes with forward migrations and regression tests; preserve existing completion history. Do not rewrite already-applied migrations to disguise the transition. See audit items A08–A12, A18–A19, A24, A28 and A30.
